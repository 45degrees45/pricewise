Features

Everything you need to track
grocery prices

No apps to install. Just send a photo to a Telegram bot and let it handle the rest.

📸

Receipt OCR

Send any receipt photo or PDF. Dual OCR pipeline extracts every line item with high accuracy, even for blurry or skewed shots.

🤖

LLM Parsing

Claude AI (or local Ollama) normalizes messy OCR text into clean structured data — item name, price, quantity, store.

📝

Text Entry

No receipt? Just type it: "Bought eggs ₹60 at DMart". The bot parses natural language just as well.

📊

Google Sheets Log

Every purchase lands in your own Google Sheet. Sort, filter, and analyze your spending history however you like.

💰

Price History

Use /best coconut to see the min, max, and average price you've ever paid for any item.

✅

Deal Checker

Use /deal 175 coconut before you buy — get an instant good / fair / expensive verdict based on your history.

How it works

Four steps from photo
to price history

1

Send a receipt

Photo, full-resolution image, or PDF — all accepted. Or describe the purchase in plain text.

2

Bot extracts items

Dual OCR (pytesseract + pdf2image) captures the text, then Claude or Ollama parses it into structured line items.

3

Review & edit

Tap through items one by one. Edit any field inline before saving — no typos reach your sheet.

4

Price history at your fingertips

Use /best and /deal commands to query your history and make smarter buying decisions.

Commands

Simple bot commands

Command What it does Example
/best <item> Price history — min, max, average across all purchases /best coconut
/deal <price> <item> Is this price good, fair, or expensive vs. your history? /deal 175 coconut
/cancel Discard the current pending receipt entry /cancel
Text message Log a purchase in plain language Bought kadachakka ₹100 at Kottayam market
